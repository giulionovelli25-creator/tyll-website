# Tyll marketing website

Rebuild of the public marketing website for Tyll (tyll.ai).

## About Tyll

Tyll is a hospitality operations platform. It helps hotels bring information from their existing systems together, identify problems, coordinate departments and track results.

## Audience and tone

- For hotel owners, general managers and hospitality groups.
- Luxurious and minimal.

## Brand elements to preserve

- Existing logo: the original, from the live tyll.ai (the two-block check and the bold "Tyll" lettering, vectors in `src/config/mark.ts`), and the owner wants its icon kept exactly as it is. On 11 Nov 2026 three alternatives were tried and all set aside the same day ("keep it to the original, it's better; maybe experiment with the font, not with the icon"): a refined check "B" (`scripts/logo/refine.mjs`), and three new directions with an identity board (`scripts/logo/concepts.mjs`, `identity.mjs`: Signature, Fold, Doors; Signature was on the local site and print for an hour). Their sheets and files stay in `assets/logo/` as a record only; `scripts/logo/icons.mjs` is not in use (it would overwrite the site's icons). Still open: the lettering. `scripts/logo/wordmarks.mjs` draws eight ways to write Tyll beside the untouched icon (`assets/logo/wordmarks/wordmarks.png`: Source Sans 3 Bold and Semibold, Inter Tight, Newsreader, Instrument Serif, Newsreader and Source Sans capitals, drawn capitals), all with fonts that are free to use and already on this computer; nothing changes on the site until the owner picks one. The owner turned all eight down ("I like the idea of a fat font like the original but maybe ... making them sharper"), so `scripts/logo/lettering.mjs` redraws the original letters themselves, same weight, size and spacing (`assets/logo/lettering/lettering.png`): 1 Cut (l tops rise to the right, pointed y tail; a T bar leaning like the check was dropped as it read as a mistake), 2 Ink traps (fine notches at inside corners), 3 Split y (the y built like the check: long stroke, short stroke, the check's gap), 4 Cut + split, 5 Split + traps. The owner picked 4, Cut + split, the same day and asked for it everywhere: it is now `WORDMARK` in `src/config/mark.ts` (five paths: T, the y's long and short strokes, l, l; the check's `SHORT` and `LONG` unchanged), so the website's header and footer, the share images (`scripts/logo/og.mjs`) and the roll-up and pamphlet (`scripts/print/lib.mjs`, rebuilt) all carry it. The logo kit `brand/tyll-logo-kit/` (and `.zip`), built by `scripts/logo/kit.mjs` from `mark.ts`, holds SVG, PNG and PDF files, a one-page sheet (`tyll-logo-sheet.pdf`) and `demo/tyll-lockup-ink-tyll.png`, a drop-in for the demo's `public/brand/` (same name, 556 x 195); the demo itself is another project and was not touched. The share images `public/media/og-*.jpg` are built by `scripts/logo/og.mjs` (11 Nov 2026: the hero photograph under a shade, the logo, and the homepage headline read from each language's `hero.title`, so they follow the site; Arabic right to left in Noto Naskh Arabic).
