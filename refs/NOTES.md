# Reference notes: the hotel section (8 Nov 2026)

The spec asked for a refs/ pack; none existed on this Mac, so it was assembled from what the owner had (Downloads, via
Finder), the TikTok, and screenshots. Third-party files stay out of git (`.gitignore`); this note is kept.

1. `01-tiktok-scroll.mp4` (the owner's WhatsApp video): one locked wide camera while the subject changes; the picture is
   the page, edge to edge; text is one short serif line, or a row of figures, set straight on the footage and only while
   the picture rests; slow, level, calm; the light warms at the end (sun low, windows glowing).
2. `02-apple-scroll-*.png` (apple.com/airpods-pro and /iphone, captured 8 Nov; stills, so they show scale, not motion):
   one idea per screen, very large headlines with lots of empty space; the move, then plateau, then text rhythm is the
   spec's section 2, which sets the timing.
3. `03-hotel-facade.png` (the owner's "Parisian Hotel After Rain"): the building. Pale limestone, slate mansard roof
   with arched dormers, wrought-iron balconies, black iron-and-glass canopy, topiary, plane trees right, wet stone
   forecourt; standing-height camera; the entrance sits at x 52%, so a phone's centre crop keeps it.
4. `04-hotel-night.png` (KE, made for this project from the same building): the night target. Deep blue sky, every
   window warm gold, canopy and lanterns glowing, long reflections on the wet stone.
5. `05-kitchen-stockcheck`: not provided. The site's licensed real kitchen photos (`05-kitchen-realism-*.jpg`) stand in
   for realism: warm practical light, real steel and skin, hands mid-task. Neither shows a tablet count, so the framing
   comes from the spec: wide, still, one person.
6. `06-bar-glass-inventory`: not provided, and the site has no bar photo (no alcohol in imagery). Realism as 05; the back
   bar holds glassware only.
7. `07-text-overlay-tiktok*.png` (frames of 01): white serif type straight on the picture, no box, legible because the
   picture behind it is calm; on daylight frames that needs the spec's bottom gradient.
8. `08-owner-section-mockup.png` (the owner's "Hotel Revenue Insights and Waste Savings"): the callout style to keep,
   white rounded cards with an icon and a label, a thin connector and a dot on the object. Its split layout and lettered
   HOTEL sign are not used.
9. `99-WRONG-current.png`: the split screen (text column left, film cropped right behind a white fade). Forbidden.
10. For the build: four locked holds, each with its subject at x 45 to 60% and the lower left calm (the desktop text sits
    there), the subject inside the central 30% of the width (all a phone shows), one lens (35 mm), one grade, no lettering.
